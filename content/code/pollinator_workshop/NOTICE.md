The exercises adapt ideas from CodeRefinery, Enrico Glerean, Bjørn Lindi, Ina
Pöhner, Jarno Rantaharju, Simon Christ, Thomas Pfau, Bahareh Tasdighi and Ashwin
Mohanan (2026), *Responsible Use of Generative AI in Assisted Coding*, version
2026-11-13, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.19335927. Copyright (c) CodeRefinery. The MQB
Collective reorganised the lesson around the revised Bloom taxonomy, developed
the pollinator examples and checks, updated tool guidance, and added a two-hour
workshop and an offline practice route. The adapted instructional material is
available under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
[Full licence text](CC-BY-4.0.txt).
[Pinned source](https://github.com/coderefinery/coding-with-ai/tree/d1a0a7049d890f5ebeb9ee6ee713a40d810a2572).

The six categories follow Krathwohl, D. R. (2002), *A Revision of Bloom's
Taxonomy: An Overview*, *Theory Into Practice*, 41(4), 212–218. The MQB
Collective developed the application to quantitative biology.
[Article](https://doi.org/10.1207/s15430421tip4104_2).

The original MQB scripts and synthetic input files carry the supplied MIT
licence. The replay candidates are prepared teaching examples. The command
output records local Python runs and has no dependency on an AI account.
